# Editing the Louma Bakery website

This site has a simple, password-protected editor. You don't need to know any
code, and you don't need a GitHub account. You log in with your email and a
password, make changes, and click **Publish**. The website updates itself a
minute or two later.

> The editor lives at **https://loumabakeryy.netlify.app/admin/**
> Bookmark that link.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open **https://loumabakeryy.netlify.app/admin/**
2. Enter the **email and password** from your invitation. (First time? See
   "Set your password" in the invitation email.)
3. You'll land on the editor, with sections down the left: **Menu**, **Images**,
   **Site settings**, and **Page text**.

You can log in from a phone, tablet, or computer.

---

## 2. The golden rule

After any change, click the **Publish** button (top right). That saves it and
puts it live. If you don't publish, nothing changes on the real site.

Changes take **about a minute or two** to appear on the live site (the site
rebuilds itself). Refresh the page if you don't see it right away.

---

## 3. Edit the menu

1. Click **Menu** → **Menu items**.
2. You'll see your categories (Breads, Pastries, etc.). Open one.
3. Inside, each **Item** has a Name, Price, Description, Tags, and Photo.
   - **Price**: type it exactly how it should look, e.g. `$4.50`.
   - **Tags**: optional little labels like `vegan`. Type one and press Enter.
   - **Add an item**: scroll to the bottom of the Items list and click
     **Add Item**.
   - **Reorder**: drag items by the handle on the left.
   - **Remove**: click the trash/remove icon on an item.
4. Click **Publish**.

---

## 4. Swap a photo

You can change a menu item's photo, or any of the main site photos.

**A menu item photo:** Menu → open the item → click the **Photo** field →
**Upload** a new image (or pick one you uploaded before) → **Publish**.

**The big site photos (hero, story, Instagram):** click **Images** → **Site
photos**. Each photo has a clear label ("Home — hero photo", "Story — founder
portrait", etc.). Click one, **Upload** a replacement, then **Publish**.

Tips for good photos:
- Use a large, sharp photo (1200 pixels wide or more). The site shrinks and
  optimizes it automatically.
- For the **hero**, use a wide (landscape) photo.
- For the **founder portrait**, use a tall (portrait) photo.

### Where to find free photos
This editor doesn't have a built-in stock-photo search. To use free,
commercial-use photos, download one from **unsplash.com** or **pexels.com**
(both free for business use), then upload it as above.

---

## 5. Change opening hours, address, phone, or links

Click **Site settings** → **Settings**.

- **Opening hours**: one row per group of days. For a closed day, put `Closed`
  in **Opens** and leave **Closes** empty.
- **Address / Phone / Email**: just type the new value.
- **Uber Eats / PayPal / Instagram links**: paste the full link. For example,
  for Uber Eats, copy your store's link from your Uber Eats dashboard and paste
  it into **Uber Eats order link**.

Click **Publish**.

> Changing the address also updates the map on the Visit page automatically.

---

## 6. Update the "Order" (Uber Eats) link

1. **Site settings** → **Settings** → **Links**.
2. Paste your store link into **Uber Eats order link**.
3. **Publish**. Every "Order" button on the site now points to it.

(Same idea for the **PayPal donate** link and **Instagram** link.)

---

## 7. Edit the words on a page

Click **Page text** → **All pages**. Each page (Home, Menu, Our Story, Mission,
Catering, Careers, Visit) is a collapsible section. Open one and edit the
**Headline**, **Intro line**, and **Paragraphs**.

A few fields are marked **internal** (like a map link or a button's `#` link) —
leave those as they are.

Click **Publish**.

---

## 8. Inviting another editor

Only an admin can do this, from the **DecapBridge** dashboard (not this editor):
DecapBridge → your site → **Collaborators** → invite by email. They'll get an
email to set a password, and then they can log in at `/admin/`.

---

## Quick reference

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Change a price or item | Menu |
| Add/remove a menu item | Menu → category → Items |
| Swap a menu photo | Menu → item → Photo |
| Change the hero / story / Instagram photos | Images |
| Change hours | Site settings → Opening hours |
| Change the Uber Eats link | Site settings → Links |
| Change the address or phone | Site settings |
| Reword a page | Page text |

**Lost or stuck?** Nothing you do here can break the site permanently — every
change is saved with a history and can be undone by your developer. When in
doubt, make the change, click **Publish**, and check the live site.
