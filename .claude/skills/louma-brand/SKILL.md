# Louma Brand

## Palette (CSS tokens)
--red #ED2739 · --burgundy #6E1F2A · --clay #B8533F · --amber-warm #C8853A
--coral #EF6E78 (new) · --navy-stitch #2B3A55 (new, for dashed sticker borders)
--ink #2A1F18 · --body #4A3D34 · --muted #7A6E62
--surface-0 #FAF5EA · --surface-1 #F5EDD8 · --surface-2 #EFE2C4 · --surface-3 #E8D4AA · --butter #FBF5D4
Rule: red is an accent, max ~3 uses per page. Burgundy is the workhorse for buttons/links.

## Type
Display/headings: Cormorant Garamond (300 italic hero, 500 headings). Small caps/eyebrows: Cormorant SC.
Body: DM Sans 300/400. Labels/prices/times: DM Mono. Eyebrows: DM Mono 10px, 0.24em, uppercase.
Hero headline clamp(40px,5.2vw,76px). H2 clamp(32px,4vw,56px). The marker wordmark is an SVG graphic, never body type.

## The sketches
baguette.svg, toast.svg = single-stroke line art → use on the scroll-linked stroke-draw animation (they draw themselves on scroll, reverse on scroll up). face.svg = the scribble-hair smiling face, the emotional center → use as logo icon, favicon, and a hero/footer ornament (static, not drawn). wordmark.svg = hand-lettered LOUMA BAKERY lockup. Sketches are red line art; on dark/coral backgrounds use butter/cream stroke.

## Hybrid principle
Keep the refined editorial typography and warm palette; let the hand-drawn sketches and face supply the warmth and personality. Don't go full cartoon. Spend boldness on the face mark and the drawing animation; keep everything around them quiet.

## Voice
Warm, concrete, unfussy. "Out of the oven by eight," not marketing-speak. Active voice. Buttons say what happens. Errors explain and offer a fix, in the brand's voice.
